Understanding Academic MOUs

What is a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)?

A Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is a formal legal agreement between Eden EdTech and an Educational Institution (College/University). It establishes a long-term strategic alliance to enhance student employability, facilitate industry-focused skill development, organize campus recruitment drives, and meet NAAC / NBA accreditation compliance requirements.
